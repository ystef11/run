Methodology
  • Hal Higdon: progressive weekly mileage and weekend long runs.
  • Pfitzinger: medium-long runs and marathon-pace blocks for experienced runners.
  • Daniels: E / T / I / R pace zones separate training stimuli.
  • Stepback + taper: down weeks every fourth week, then a gradual pre-race reduction.
Primary sources & scientific basis
  • Higdon: Hal Higdon's official marathon programs (Novice / Intermediate / Advanced).
  • Pfitzinger: Pfitzinger & Douglas, Advanced Marathoning (Human Kinetics, 4th ed.) — source of the medium-long and marathon-pace blocks.
  • Daniels: Jack Daniels, Daniels' Running Formula (Human Kinetics, 4th ed., 2022) — the E / T / I / R pace zones and VDOT system.
  • Why is most volume at Easy pace? Seiler & Kjerland 2006 (Scand J Med Sci Sports; PMID 16430681) found elite endurance athletes train ~75% at low intensity and only 15–20% hard — "polarized" training. That is why this generator keeps most mileage easy and uses quality work sparingly.
