Hill repeats help build leg strength and mental toughness. Attack the uphill and recover on the way down. Hill running is an excellent way to build strength and power, which are essential for tackling challenging courses. Hills are your secret weapon, and today you'll learn how to harness their power. As you run, focus on driving your knees, pumping your arms, and maintaining a strong posture. Complete your hill intervals at a 3% - 6% incline using the active recovery time to jog back down the hill.
